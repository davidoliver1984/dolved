# Compliments and Feedback Procedure

Document family: `family.complaints.feedback`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Compliments and suggestions are logged separately from complaints but reviewed together for service-learning themes. Anonymous feedback is accepted and should record the channel, service and subject without attempting to identify the sender.

Feedback that alleges harm, abuse or a service failure is reclassified and routed through the appropriate safeguarding, incident or complaints process.
