# Overtime and Additional Hours Procedure

Document family: `family.payroll.overtime`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Overtime or additional hours must be authorised by the duty manager before they are worked, except where immediate action is necessary to protect safety. The employee records the actual start, finish and unpaid break in the rota system.

Authorisation confirms business need; it does not override working-time or safe-staffing controls. Estimated hours must be corrected to actual hours before payroll approval.
