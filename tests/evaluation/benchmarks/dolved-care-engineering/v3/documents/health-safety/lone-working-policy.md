# Lone Working Risk Control Policy

Document family: `family.health-safety.lone-working`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 May 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Managers assess travel, location, task, communication, known behaviour and emergency-access risks before lone work is allocated. Controls may include scheduled welfare calls, paired visits, personal alarms or a decision that the task cannot be completed alone.

If a welfare check is missed, the separate welfare-check procedure governs escalation. Staff must not treat a mobile phone signal as the only control where coverage is unreliable.
