# Safety Equipment Inspection Schedule

Document family: `family.health-safety.equipment-checks`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Equipment | User check | Formal inspection |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile hoist | Before each use | Every 6 months |
| Passenger lift | Visual check daily | Thorough examination every 6 months |
| Evacuation chair | Monthly | Annual service |
| Portable electrical equipment | Before use | Risk-based test interval |
| Fire extinguisher | Monthly visual check | Annual service |

An inspection interval never authorises continued use of visibly damaged equipment.
