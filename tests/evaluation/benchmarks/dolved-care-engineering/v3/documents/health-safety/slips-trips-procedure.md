# Slips, Trips and Falls Procedure

Document family: `family.health-safety.slips-trips`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 June 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Spills are guarded and cleaned immediately using the appropriate method. Warning signs are a temporary control and must be removed when the floor is dry. Damaged flooring, poor lighting and trailing cables are reported and isolated where they create immediate risk.

After a fall, staff protect the person, obtain clinical assessment as required and avoid moving them unless remaining in place creates greater danger. The incident record and the person's falls plan are reviewed separately.
