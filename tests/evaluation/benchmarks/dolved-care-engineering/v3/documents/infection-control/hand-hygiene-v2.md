# Hand Hygiene Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.hand-hygiene`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Use liquid soap and water when hands are visibly dirty, after caring for a person with vomiting or diarrhoea, and whenever spore-forming infection is suspected. Alcohol hand rub is suitable for routine decontamination only when hands are visibly clean and the organism is not one for which soap and water is required.

Staff clean all hand surfaces for at least 20 seconds, dry thoroughly and keep wrists free of watches during direct care.
