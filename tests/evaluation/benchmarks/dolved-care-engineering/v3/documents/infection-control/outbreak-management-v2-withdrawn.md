# Respiratory Outbreak Management Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.outbreak-management`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 October 2025
Withdrawn: 1 June 2026
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Initial response

Two or more epidemiologically linked respiratory symptoms require the registered
manager to open an outbreak record, seek clinical advice and begin twice-daily
monitoring of new symptoms.

## Withdrawal consequence

This version attained authority on 1 October 2025 and was withdrawn on 1 June
2026. Its withdrawal closed its authority window. Version 1.0 does not become
authoritative again, so no current version exists at the benchmark clock.

## Protective measures

Staff use the current PPE selection reference, increase cleaning of shared touch
points and document individual decisions about visits and communal activities.
