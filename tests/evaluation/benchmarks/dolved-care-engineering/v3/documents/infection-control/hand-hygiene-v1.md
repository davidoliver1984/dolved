# Hand Hygiene Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.hand-hygiene`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Use liquid soap and water when hands are visibly dirty and after caring for a person with vomiting or diarrhoea. Alcohol hand rub may be used for routine decontamination when hands are visibly clean. Staff wash for at least 20 seconds and dry with a disposable towel.
