# Respiratory Infection PPE Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.respiratory-ppe`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 October 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

For routine close personal care of a person with respiratory symptoms, staff wear gloves and an apron where exposure to body fluids is anticipated and a fluid-resistant surgical mask where the current risk assessment requires it. Eye protection is added when splashing is reasonably foreseeable.

An aerosol-generating procedure requires a fit-tested respirator, long-sleeved gown, gloves and eye protection. The generic PPE table remains relevant, but this procedure supplies the respiratory context.
