# Midlands Community Specimen Transport Procedure

Document family: `family.infection-control.midlands-community-specimen-transport`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2026
Applies to: Midlands Region community services
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Community staff must close the specimen in its labelled primary container,
place it in a leak-resistant secondary pouch with absorbent material, and put
the pouch inside the rigid, lidded specimen carrier. The request form stays in
the carrier's separate document pocket and must not share the specimen pouch.

Before handover, staff record the collection time and receiving service on the
transport log. The closed carrier is handed directly to an approved courier;
it must not be left unattended at reception or in a vehicle.
