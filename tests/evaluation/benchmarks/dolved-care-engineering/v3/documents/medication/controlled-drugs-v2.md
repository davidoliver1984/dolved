# Controlled Drugs Procedure

Document family: `family.medication.controlled-drugs`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 July 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

An authorised staff member and an independent authorised witness must observe each controlled-drug administration. Both sign the register at the time of administration; a later countersignature is prohibited. The running balance is checked against physical stock after every entry.

A discrepancy must be escalated immediately, not at the end of the shift. The shift leader secures the cabinet, repeats the balance with a second witness and informs the registered manager. Where diversion or harm is suspected, safeguarding and incident-reporting procedures also apply.

Returned or expired controlled drugs are stored in the designated segregated section until pharmacy collection. Destruction requires the authorised witness and a contemporaneous register entry.
