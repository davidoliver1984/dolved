# Medicines Storage and Temperature Procedure

Document family: `family.medication.storage`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Routine monitoring

The medicines refrigerator must be kept between 2°C and 8°C. The minimum,
maximum and current temperature must be recorded at the start of each day by a
trained member of staff. After recording, the thermometer memory is reset.

| Reading | Required response |
|---|---|
| 2°C to 8°C | Record the reading and continue normal use |
| Below 2°C | Quarantine affected stock and contact the supplying pharmacist |
| Above 8°C | Quarantine affected stock and contact the supplying pharmacist |
| No reliable reading | Treat the stock as potentially out of range |

## Out-of-range response

When any reading is outside 2°C to 8°C, staff must label the affected medicines “QUARANTINED — DO NOT USE”, keep them secure, and record the earliest possible time the excursion began.
The shift leader must contact the supplying pharmacist for medicine-specific stability advice.
No quarantined medicine may be returned
to use until that advice is recorded.

## Administration dependency

The administration policy requires staff to check the latest refrigerator record
before removing refrigerated medicine. An out-of-range medicine must not be
administered merely because its packaging appears undamaged.
