# Medication Error Reporting Form

Document family: `family.medication.errors`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 May 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Required field | Information to record |
|---|---|
| Person supported | Name and stable care-record identifier |
| Incident | Medicine, dose, route, scheduled time and what occurred |
| Immediate safety | Observations, clinical advice and protective action |
| MAR | Relevant entry or exception code |
| Notifications | Shift leader, registered manager, prescriber or pharmacy |
| Reporter | Name, role, date and time |

Complete the form after immediate safety actions. Do not delay clinical advice while gathering administrative detail. Attach no photographs containing unrelated personal information.
