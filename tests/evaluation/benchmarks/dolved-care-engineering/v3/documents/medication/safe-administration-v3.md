## Scheduled change notice

This approved version is scheduled for 1 October 2026 and is not authoritative
before that date. Until then, version 2.0 remains the current policy.

## Administration record

The member of staff must sign the electronic or paper Medication Administration
Record immediately after observing administration. In addition to the existing
exception note, every omitted time-critical dose will require the electronic
incident reference to be entered on the MAR before the end of the shift.

## Digital outage

During an authorised electronic MAR outage, staff must use the numbered downtime
pack and reconcile every entry when the system is restored. Photographs of a MAR
screen are not an authorised substitute record.
