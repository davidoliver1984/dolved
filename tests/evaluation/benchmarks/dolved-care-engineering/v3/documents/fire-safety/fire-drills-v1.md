# Fire Drill and Debrief Procedure

Document family: `family.fire.drills`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Each service completes a fire drill at least every six months. Drills should rotate across shifts so night staff participate at least annually. The record includes alarm time, evacuation duration, staff present, simulated conditions and actions agreed.

The registered manager reviews the drill within five working days and assigns actions with owners and dates.
