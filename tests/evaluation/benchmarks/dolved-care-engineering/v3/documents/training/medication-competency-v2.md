# Medication Competency Assessment

Document family: `family.training.medication-competency`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 January 2026
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Assessment element | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| MAR reading | Correctly identifies medicine, dose, route and time |
| Administration | Four directly observed medication rounds, including one controlled-drug round where the role requires it |
| PRN | Explains indication, minimum interval and records outcome |

Competency is reassessed every twelve months, after a significant medication error, and after six months away from medication duties.
