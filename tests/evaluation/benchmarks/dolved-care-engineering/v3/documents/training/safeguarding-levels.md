# Safeguarding Training Levels Reference

Document family: `family.training.safeguarding`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Role | Required learning | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All employees | Safeguarding adults awareness | Every 12 months |
| Care and support staff | Applied safeguarding adults | Every 12 months |
| Registered managers | Safeguarding decision-making and referrals | Every 24 months plus annual update |
| Board members | Governance oversight | Every 24 months |

Role changes trigger a review of the required level; the next annual refresher is not always sufficient.
