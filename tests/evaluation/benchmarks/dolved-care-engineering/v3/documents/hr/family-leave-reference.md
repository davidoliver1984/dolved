# Family Leave Reference Table

Document family: `family.hr.family-leave`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Leave type | Initial notice route | Internal contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maternity or adoption | Tell the manager and upload evidence to the HR portal | People Team |
| Paternity | Submit the family-leave form | People Team |
| Unpaid parental leave | Give at least 21 days' notice | Line manager |
| Emergency dependants leave | Notify the duty manager as soon as practicable | Line manager |

This table summarises internal notification routes and does not replace statutory eligibility rules.
