# Safer Recruitment Policy

Document family: `family.hr.recruitment`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Care roles require identity and right-to-work checks, a complete employment history, two satisfactory references and the appropriate Disclosure and Barring Service check before unsupervised work.

A risk-assessed supervised start may be authorised only by the Director of People where the DBS update-service status is confirmed and all other checks are complete. A pending first-time DBS application does not permit unsupervised work.
