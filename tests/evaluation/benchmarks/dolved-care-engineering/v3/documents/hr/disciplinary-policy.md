# Disciplinary Policy

Document family: `family.hr.disciplinary`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

An investigatory meeting establishes facts and is not itself a disciplinary hearing. The employee must receive the allegation, evidence and possible consequences in writing before a formal hearing.

Suspension is a neutral precaution, not a disciplinary sanction, and must be reviewed every ten working days. Employees may be accompanied at a formal hearing by a trade union representative or workplace colleague.
