# Grievance Procedure

Document family: `family.hr.grievance`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Employees should normally raise a grievance in writing with their line manager. If the grievance concerns that manager, it must be sent to the next manager or People Team.

A grievance meeting should normally take place within seven working days. An appeal must be submitted within five working days of the written outcome.
