# Sickness Absence Procedure

Document family: `family.hr.sickness-absence`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Employees must telephone their line manager personally at least one hour before the shift begins. A text message is not sufficient unless an emergency prevents a call.

A return-to-work conversation is completed after every absence. A fit note is required from the eighth calendar day of continuous absence.
