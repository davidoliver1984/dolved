# DoLS Application Reference

Document family: `family.safeguarding.dols`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Situation | Required route |
|---|---|
| Planned restrictions may amount to deprivation of liberty | Submit a standard authorisation request before implementation where practicable |
| Deprivation is already occurring and cannot safely wait | Submit urgent and standard requests together |
| Person is in hospital | Follow the hospital managing authority route |
| Person is supported in their own home | Seek legal advice; the care-home DoLS route does not apply |

DoLS means Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards. A DoLS authorisation does not remove the need for decision-specific capacity and best-interests decisions.
