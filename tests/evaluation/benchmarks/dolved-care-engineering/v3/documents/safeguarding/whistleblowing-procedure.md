# Whistleblowing Procedure

Document family: `family.safeguarding.whistleblowing`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

An employee may raise a concern with their line manager, the safeguarding lead or the confidential speaking-up contact. If the concern involves the line manager, the employee should use either of the latter routes. Immediate danger or suspected crime must be reported through the emergency and safeguarding routes without waiting for an internal meeting.

A person raising a genuine concern in good faith will not be disadvantaged, even when an investigation does not substantiate it. Deliberately false allegations are addressed separately and must not be assumed merely because evidence is incomplete.
