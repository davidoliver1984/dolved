# Outbreak Visiting Restrictions Procedure

Document family: `family.visitors.outbreak-restrictions`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 October 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

During a declared outbreak, routine indoor visiting is paused for all residents. Essential end-of-life visits require manager approval and visitors must follow the PPE instructions given on arrival.

The manager reviews restrictions every seven days with infection-control advice.
