# Visitors and Contractors Procedure

Document family: `family.visitors.general`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Arrival and identity

All visitors must sign in at reception and wear the issued visitor badge.
A contractor must additionally show photographic identification and be met by the
employee responsible for the work.

## Fire information

Reception staff tell visitors how to recognise the fire alarm and direct them to
the assembly arrangement for that location.
Visitors must not use lifts during an evacuation and must remain with their host or follow staff instructions.

## Confidentiality and infection precautions

Visitors must not photograph records, staff or people supported without explicit
authorisation. They must follow any displayed infection-control precautions and
postpone non-essential visits when they have symptoms of a transmissible illness.

## Departure

Visitors sign out, return their badge and notify reception if their host is no
longer available. The sign-in record is used during emergency roll calls.
