# Records Retention Schedule

Document family: `family.gdpr.retention`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 May 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Record | Retention trigger | Retention period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsuccessful applicant file | Recruitment decision | 6 months |
| Employee personnel file | Employment ends | 6 years |
| Adult social care record | Care ends | 8 years |
| Visitor sign-in record | Date of visit | 12 months |

A legal hold suspends routine deletion for the records named in the hold.
