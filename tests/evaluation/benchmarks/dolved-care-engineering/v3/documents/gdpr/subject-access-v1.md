# Subject Access Request Procedure

Document family: `family.gdpr.subject-access`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

A subject access request may be written or verbal and does not need to mention data-protection law. Forward it to the Privacy Team on the day received.

Identity must be verified proportionately. The response deadline is one calendar month from receipt, paused only while necessary identity clarification is outstanding.
