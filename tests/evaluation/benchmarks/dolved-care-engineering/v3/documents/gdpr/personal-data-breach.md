# Personal Data Breach Procedure

Document family: `family.gdpr.breach`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Preserve the message, screenshot or envelope that demonstrates the incident, but do not forward personal data more widely to prove what happened. Notify the Privacy Team immediately with the time discovered, data involved, recipients and containment already attempted.

Only the Data Protection Officer decides whether the Information Commissioner's Office must be notified within seventy-two hours. Staff must not contact affected people before the coordinated risk assessment unless urgent action is needed to prevent harm.
