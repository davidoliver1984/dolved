# Flexible Working Procedure

Document family: `family.hr.flexible-working`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 15 April 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Employees may make a statutory flexible-working request from their first day of employment. The written request must describe the proposed change and preferred start date.

A manager must consult before refusing a request and complete the process, including any appeal, within two months unless an extension is agreed. Approval is not automatic where safe staffing cannot be maintained.
