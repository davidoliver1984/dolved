# Midlands Lone Worker Welfare Check Procedure

Document family: `family.hr.lone-worker-welfare`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 May 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

This procedure applies to the Midlands Region, including the Coventry community team. A lone community worker checks in through the rota system at the start and end of each visit.

If an end-of-visit check-in is fifteen minutes overdue, the coordinator calls the worker. If there is no response after a further ten minutes, the coordinator follows the emergency escalation contact and considers police assistance according to the known risk.
