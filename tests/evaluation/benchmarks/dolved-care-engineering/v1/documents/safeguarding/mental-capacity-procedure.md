# Mental Capacity and Best Interests Procedure

Document family: `family.safeguarding.mental-capacity`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Capacity is assessed for the particular decision at the time it must be made. Staff must first provide practicable support, including accessible information, familiar communication and an appropriate time or setting. An unwise decision does not by itself demonstrate lack of capacity.

When a person cannot make the specific decision, the best-interests record must consider their wishes, feelings, beliefs, relevant alternatives and the views of appropriate others. The least restrictive workable option is preferred. A blanket statement that a person “lacks capacity” is not an adequate assessment.
