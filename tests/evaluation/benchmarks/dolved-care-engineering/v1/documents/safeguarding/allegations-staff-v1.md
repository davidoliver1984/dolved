# Allegations Against Staff Procedure

Document family: `family.safeguarding.allegations-staff`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 October 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

The registered manager protects the person supported, preserves evidence and informs the safeguarding lead. The employee who is the subject of an allegation must not be asked to investigate or contact witnesses. Suspension is considered only after an initial risk assessment and is not an automatic finding of fault.

The manager informs HR within one working day and coordinates external notifications with safeguarding and police where required.
