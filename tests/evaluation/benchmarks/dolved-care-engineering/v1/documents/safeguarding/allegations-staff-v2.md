# Allegations Against Staff Procedure

Document family: `family.safeguarding.allegations-staff`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 October 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

The registered manager takes immediate protective action, preserves evidence and informs the safeguarding lead without waiting for an employment decision. The employee must not investigate, approach witnesses or access records beyond what their current duties require.

HR must be informed immediately rather than within one working day. A documented multi-agency strategy determines whether internal fact-finding can begin. Suspension remains a neutral risk-control option, not an automatic response or finding of fault.
