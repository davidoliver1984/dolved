# Adult Safeguarding Reporting Policy

Document family: `family.safeguarding.adult-reporting`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 July 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Immediate protection

If anyone is in immediate danger, staff must call the emergency services and
take proportionate steps to reduce further harm. Evidence must be preserved;
staff should not confront an alleged perpetrator or conduct their own interview.

## Reporting

Every safeguarding concern must be reported to the shift leader or registered manager immediately and recorded before the staff member finishes the shift.
The registered manager decides whether to notify the local authority safeguarding
team, but a manager's absence must never delay immediate protective action.

## Speaking up

Staff may report directly to the safeguarding lead when the concern involves
their manager. No employee will be disadvantaged for raising a genuine concern
in good faith.
