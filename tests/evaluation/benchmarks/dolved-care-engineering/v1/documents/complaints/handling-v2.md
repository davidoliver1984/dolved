# Complaints Handling Policy

Document family: `family.complaints.handling`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 July 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Staff acknowledge a complaint within two working days. The investigating manager agrees a response plan with the complainant and aims to issue the written outcome within fifteen working days.

A complaint may be verbal, written or made through an advocate. Staff must record it immediately without requiring a form. Any delay is explained before the agreed response date.
