# Fire Drill and Debrief Procedure

Document family: `family.fire.drills`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Each residential service completes a fire drill at least quarterly; community and office services complete one at least every six months. Drills rotate across shifts and every residential night team must participate at least annually.

The drill record includes alarm time, compartment or route tested, evacuation duration, staffing level, people requiring assistance and actions. The registered manager holds a debrief within two working days and verifies completion of critical actions within ten working days.
