# Personal Emergency Evacuation Plan

Document family: `family.fire.peep`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Person | Name and care-record identifier |
| Assistance | Mobility, communication and sensory support |
| Route | Primary and alternative evacuation routes |
| Equipment | Evacuation aid and trained operators |
| Staffing | Minimum assistance and role allocation |
| Review | Six-month review date or earlier trigger |

PEEP means Personal Emergency Evacuation Plan. The plan must be available to staff without displaying unnecessary personal information in public areas.
