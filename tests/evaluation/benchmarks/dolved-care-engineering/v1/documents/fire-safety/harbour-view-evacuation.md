# Harbour View Fire Evacuation Procedure

Document family: `family.fire.harbour-view-evacuation`
Version: 1.0
Applies to: Harbour View only
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Local building arrangement

Harbour View is divided into three fire compartments. Staff initially move each
person beyond the nearest fire-resisting doors and continue evacuation only when
the incident requires it.

## Harbour View assembly point

At Harbour View, staff and visitors assemble in the east garden beyond the laundry gate, not in the front visitor car park.
The nurse in charge takes the
red emergency folder from reception and meets the fire and rescue service at the
main entrance.

## Relationship to the regional procedure

The South West regional procedure remains applicable to Harbour View. This
site-specific procedure supplies the local assembly point and building facts;
it does not make the regional alarm and emergency-call duties irrelevant.
