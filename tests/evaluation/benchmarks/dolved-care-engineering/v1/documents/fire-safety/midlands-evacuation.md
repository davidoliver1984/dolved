# Midlands Emergency Evacuation Procedure

Document family: `family.fire.midlands-evacuation`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

This procedure applies to Oakfield Lodge and Willow Bank Community Service. Activate the alarm, call 999 and follow the local evacuation strategy. Oakfield staff use progressive horizontal evacuation; community staff leave by the nearest safe exit.

The regional fallback assembly point is the signed delivery-area muster point. The on-call manager is informed after emergency services have been contacted, never instead of calling 999.
