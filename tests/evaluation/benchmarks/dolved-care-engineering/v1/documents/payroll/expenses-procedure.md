# Employee Expenses Procedure

Document family: `family.payroll.expenses`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Expense claims require an itemised receipt and must be submitted within thirty calendar days of the purchase. The cost centre manager approves business purpose; Payroll checks tax and evidence requirements.

Alcohol, personal subscriptions and ordinary home-to-work travel are not reimbursable. A missing receipt declaration may be used only where replacement evidence cannot reasonably be obtained.
