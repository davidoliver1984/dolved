# Subject Access Request Procedure

Document family: `family.gdpr.subject-access`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 December 2026 (scheduled)
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

A subject access request may be written, verbal or submitted through the privacy portal. Frontline staff will log the request in the portal within one working day.

Identity will be verified proportionately. The response deadline remains one calendar month from receipt.
