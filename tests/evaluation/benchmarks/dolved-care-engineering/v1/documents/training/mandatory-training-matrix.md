# Mandatory Training Matrix

Document family: `family.training.matrix`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 January 2026
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Role or activity | Learning requirement | Refresher interval |
|---|---|---|
| All employees | Adult safeguarding awareness | 12 months |
| Care worker | Moving and handling | 12 months |
| Care worker | Infection prevention and control | 12 months |
| Staff who administer medicines | Medicines administration and observed competency | 12 months |
| Fire marshal | Fire marshal practical course | 24 months |
| Office-only employee | General fire safety awareness | 24 months |

Training must be completed before the recorded expiry date. A manager may require
an earlier reassessment following an incident, prolonged absence or material
change in duties.
