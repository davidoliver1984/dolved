# Medication Competency Assessment

Document family: `family.training.medication-competency`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 January 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Assessment element | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| MAR reading | Correctly identifies medicine, dose and time |
| Administration | Three directly observed medication rounds |
| PRN | Explains indication and records outcome |

Competency is reassessed every twelve months and after a significant medication error.
