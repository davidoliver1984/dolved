# New Starter Induction Programme

Document family: `family.training.induction`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 November 2026 (scheduled)
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

New starters will complete corporate induction within their first five working days. Before working without direct supervision they will complete local fire orientation, safeguarding awareness, infection-control essentials, information governance and role-specific moving-and-handling instruction.

The manager will review the digital induction passport before the employee's first unsupervised shift.
