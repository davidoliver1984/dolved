# New Starter Induction Programme

Document family: `family.training.induction`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 January 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

New starters complete corporate induction within their first ten working days. Before working without direct supervision they must complete local fire orientation, safeguarding awareness, infection-control essentials and role-specific moving-and-handling instruction.

The manager records induction completion in the learning system and resolves any missed topic before the employee's first unsupervised shift.
