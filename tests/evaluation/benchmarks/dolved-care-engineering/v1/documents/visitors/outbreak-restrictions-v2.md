# Outbreak Visiting Restrictions Procedure

Document family: `family.visitors.outbreak-restrictions`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 October 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

During a declared outbreak, each resident may continue to receive one nominated essential visitor unless an individual risk assessment identifies a specific reason to pause that visit. End-of-life visiting is supported with proportionate infection-control measures.

The manager reviews restrictions every forty-eight hours with infection-control advice. Blanket suspension of all visits is not the default response.
