# PPE Selection Reference

Document family: `family.infection.ppe`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Task | Gloves | Apron or gown | Face protection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal care without exposure risk | Risk assess | Apron if clothing may be contaminated | Not routinely required |
| Contact with blood or body fluids | Yes | Apron | Eye/face protection if splash risk |
| Cleaning vomit or diarrhoea | Yes | Apron | Eye/face protection if splash risk |
| Respiratory aerosol-generating procedure | Yes | Long-sleeved gown | Respirator and eye protection |

PPE is selected for the task and exposure risk, not solely for a diagnosis.
