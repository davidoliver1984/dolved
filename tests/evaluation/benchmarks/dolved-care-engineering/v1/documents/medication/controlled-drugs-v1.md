# Controlled Drugs Procedure

Document family: `family.medication.controlled-drugs`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 June 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Two authorised members of staff must witness every controlled-drug administration and sign the controlled-drugs register immediately. The running balance is checked after each entry. A discrepancy is reported to the registered manager before the end of the shift and the stock is secured while the check is repeated.

Expired stock remains in the controlled-drugs cabinet, clearly segregated, until collected by the supplying pharmacy. Staff must not destroy controlled drugs without an authorised witness and the required record.
