# Medication Fridge Monitoring Reference

Document family: `family.medication.fridge-reference`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Current reading | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 2°C to 8°C inclusive | In range | Record and continue normal use |
| 1.9°C or below | Too cold | Quarantine stock and seek pharmacy advice |
| 8.1°C or above | Too warm | Quarantine stock and seek pharmacy advice |
| Missing or unreliable | Unknown | Treat as potentially out of range |

This quick reference does not authorise staff to return stock to use. The full storage and temperature procedure governs investigation and release from quarantine.
