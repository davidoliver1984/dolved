# COSHH Assessment Procedure

Document family: `family.health-safety.coshh`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Before a hazardous substance is introduced, the manager obtains the current safety data sheet and completes a COSHH assessment covering exposure routes, storage, handling, PPE, spill response and disposal. Manufacturer instructions are evidence, not a substitute for the workplace assessment.

Assessments are reviewed annually and immediately after a formulation, task or control changes. Decanted products must retain an identifiable label; drink containers must never be used.
