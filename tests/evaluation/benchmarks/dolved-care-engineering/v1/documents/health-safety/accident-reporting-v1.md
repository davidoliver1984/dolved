# Accident and Incident Reporting Procedure

Document family: `family.health-safety.accident-reporting`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 July 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Staff first make the area safe, provide first aid and obtain emergency assistance. Every accident, near miss or dangerous occurrence is recorded before the end of the shift. The manager reviews whether the event may be reportable under RIDDOR and contacts the health and safety lead within two working days.
