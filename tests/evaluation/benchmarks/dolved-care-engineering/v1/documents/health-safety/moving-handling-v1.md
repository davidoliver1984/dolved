# Moving and Handling Policy

Document family: `family.health-safety.moving-handling`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 October 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Staff follow the person's moving-and-handling plan and use the named equipment. Two staff are required for every full-body hoist transfer. Any equipment defect is reported and the equipment removed from use.

The plan is reviewed annually or after a fall, material mobility change or equipment change.
