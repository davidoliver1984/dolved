# Duty of Candour Procedure

Document family: `family.complaints.duty-candour`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

When a notifiable safety incident is identified, the registered manager ensures the relevant person is told promptly, receives a truthful account of known facts, an apology and an explanation of further enquiries.

The initial notification is followed by written confirmation. Saying sorry is not an admission of legal liability and must never be withheld for that reason.
