# PRN Medicines Protocol

Document family: `family.medication.prn`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

PRN means medicine prescribed to be given when required. Before offering a PRN medicine, staff must check the person's current protocol for the indication, minimum interval, maximum dose in 24 hours and non-medicine measures to try first. A PRN entry on the MAR is not, by itself, enough information to administer safely.

Staff record the reason for the dose, time, quantity and observed outcome. If the medicine is ineffective, requested unusually often or refused when symptoms remain concerning, the shift leader must review the care plan and obtain clinical advice. PRN medicine must never be used for staff convenience or as an unrecorded response to distress.
