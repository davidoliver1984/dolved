# Covert Administration of Medicines Policy

Document family: `family.medication.covert`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 August 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Covert administration means disguising medicine in food or drink without the person's knowledge. It is never justified merely because a person refuses medicine. A decision requires a decision-specific assessment that the person lacks capacity, a recorded best-interests process, prescriber agreement and pharmacy advice confirming the proposed method will not alter the medicine unsafely.

The plan must name each medicine, method, review date and circumstances requiring reconsideration. Staff follow the current authorised plan exactly and record every administration on the MAR. If the person regains capacity, the covert plan cannot continue on the authority of an earlier assessment.
