# Self-Administration Risk Assessment

Document family: `family.medication.self-administration`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 September 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Assessment area | Record |
|---|---|
| Understanding | Purpose, dose and timing understood by the person |
| Capacity | Decision-specific capacity assessment where doubt exists |
| Physical ability | Can open packaging and use the prescribed route safely |
| Storage | Secure storage available without preventing agreed access |
| Support | Prompting, ordering or monitoring assistance agreed |
| Review | Review date and triggers for earlier reassessment |

Self-administration is an individual choice supported by proportionate risk management. A diagnosis alone does not determine capacity or remove the person's involvement.
