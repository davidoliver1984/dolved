# Safe Administration of Medicines Policy

Document family: `family.medication.administration`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 January 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Purpose

This policy describes the minimum controls for staff who administer medicines
to people supported by Alderbridge Care Group. Only staff whose training and
competency assessment are current may administer medicines without direct
supervision.

## Administration record

The member of staff administering a medicine must sign the Medication
Administration Record (MAR) immediately after the person has taken the dose.
The signature must never be added before administration. Where a dose is not
given, staff must enter the correct MAR exception code and add a concise note.

## Checks before administration

Before giving a medicine, staff must confirm the person's identity, medicine, dose, route and scheduled time against the MAR.
Staff must also check the label
is legible and the medicine is within its expiry date. For medicines stored in
a refrigerator, the most recent recorded temperature must be within the
approved range before the medicine is removed for administration.

## Missed or refused doses

A refused dose must be recorded using code R.
Staff should explain the purpose of the medicine without coercion and notify the
shift leader when the care plan requires escalation. A dose that is unavailable
must be recorded using code U.

## PRN medicines

Before administering a PRN medicine, staff must consult the person's current
PRN protocol. The reason, dose and observed outcome must be recorded.

## Audit

The registered manager arranges a monthly MAR audit. Any unexplained gap is
treated as a medication incident and reviewed under the medication error
procedure.
