# Moving and Handling Policy

Document family: `family.health-safety.moving-handling`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 October 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Staff follow the individual moving-and-handling plan and use the named equipment. The assessment, rather than a universal rule, determines whether one or two trained staff are required for a hoist transfer. Where the plan requires two, the transfer must not begin with only one.

Defective equipment is labelled “DO NOT USE”, isolated and reported immediately. Plans are reviewed every six months and after a fall, mobility change, pain, equipment change or unsafe transfer.
