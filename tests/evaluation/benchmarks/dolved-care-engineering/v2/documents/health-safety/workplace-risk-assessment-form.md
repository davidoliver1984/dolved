# Workplace Risk Assessment Form

Document family: `family.health-safety.risk-assessment`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Required field | Record |
|---|---|
| Hazard | What could cause harm |
| People affected | Employees, people supported, visitors or contractors |
| Existing controls | Measures already operating |
| Risk rating | Likelihood and consequence before further action |
| Further action | Owner and completion date |
| Residual risk | Rating after controls |
| Review | Planned date and earlier triggers |

Generic phrases such as “staff to take care” are not adequate controls.
