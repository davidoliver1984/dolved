# Accident and Incident Reporting Procedure

Document family: `family.health-safety.accident-reporting`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 July 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Staff first protect people, provide first aid and preserve relevant evidence. Accidents, near misses and dangerous occurrences are recorded before the end of the shift. The manager must notify the health and safety lead within one working day where RIDDOR may apply; the health and safety lead owns the formal reporting decision.

Photographs must exclude unrelated people and records. Serious incidents require an immediate verbal escalation in addition to the electronic report.
