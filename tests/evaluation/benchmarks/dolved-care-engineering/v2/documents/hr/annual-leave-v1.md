# Annual Leave Policy

Document family: `family.hr.annual-leave`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 January 2023
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave including public holidays. Requests for five or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

Managers should respond within ten working days. No more than five unused days may be carried into the next leave year with written approval.
