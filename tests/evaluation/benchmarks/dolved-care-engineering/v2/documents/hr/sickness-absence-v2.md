# Sickness Absence Procedure

Document family: `family.hr.sickness-absence`
Version: 2.0
Effective: 1 April 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Employees must telephone the duty manager personally at least two hours before the shift begins. A text or messaging-app notification is not sufficient unless an emergency prevents a call.

A return-to-work conversation is completed after every absence. A fit note is required from the eighth calendar day of continuous absence. Night workers who cannot reach the duty manager must call the on-call number and leave a callback number.
