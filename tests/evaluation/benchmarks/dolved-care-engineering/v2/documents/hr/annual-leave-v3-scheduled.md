# Annual Leave Policy

Document family: `family.hr.annual-leave`
Version: 3.0
Effective: 1 October 2026 (scheduled)
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Full-time employees will receive 31 days of paid annual leave including public holidays. Requests for five or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least eight weeks in advance.

Managers will respond within five working days. Up to three unused days may be carried into the next leave year with written approval.
