# Respiratory Outbreak Management Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.outbreak-management`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 October 2024
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Initial response

Two or more linked respiratory symptoms must be escalated to the registered
manager. The manager begins a line list, seeks clinical advice and reviews group
activities and visiting arrangements.

## Protective measures

Staff reinforce hand hygiene, ventilation, cleaning and appropriate PPE. People
supported should receive individual explanations of any temporary restrictions.

## Closure

The service records the date symptoms began and the date the outbreak is declared
over. Learning is reviewed at the next governance meeting.
