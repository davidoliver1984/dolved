# Clinical Waste Segregation Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.clinical-waste`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 May 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Waste | Receptacle |
|---|---|
| Non-infectious offensive hygiene waste | Yellow-and-black striped bag |
| Infectious non-medicinal waste | Orange bag |
| Sharps without medicinal contamination | Orange-lidded sharps container |
| Sharps contaminated with medicine | Yellow-lidded sharps container |

Bags are closed when three-quarters full and labelled with the service identifier. Waste must never be transferred by hand from one bag to another.
