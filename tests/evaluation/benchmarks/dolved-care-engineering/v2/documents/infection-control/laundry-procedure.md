# Infected Linen and Laundry Procedure

Document family: `family.infection.laundry`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 June 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

Do not shake used linen. Wear task-appropriate PPE and place linen contaminated with body fluids directly into a water-soluble inner bag and the designated outer bag at the point of use. The bag is labelled and transported by the dirty route.

Clean and used linen remain physically separated. Staff perform hand hygiene after removing PPE and after handling the outer bag.
