# Resident Isolation Risk Assessment

Document family: `family.infection.isolation`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 October 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Assessment area | Record |
|---|---|
| Infection risk | Organism, symptoms, transmission route and clinical advice |
| Capacity and consent | Decision-specific assessment and person's wishes |
| Harms of restriction | Distress, mobility, nutrition, social contact and liberty |
| Alternatives | Cohorting, ventilation, PPE and adjusted activities |
| Support plan | Communication, meaningful activity and review frequency |
| End point | Clear criteria for reducing or ending restrictions |

Isolation is never imposed solely because it is convenient for staffing.
