# North West Emergency Evacuation Procedure

Document family: `family.fire.north-west-evacuation`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 March 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

This procedure applies to Riverside House and Moorland View. Activate the alarm, call 999 and begin progressive horizontal evacuation. Unless a site plan states otherwise, the regional assembly point is the rear service-yard gate.

The senior person present takes the yellow emergency folder and reports anyone unaccounted for to the fire and rescue service. Staff must not re-enter until authorised.
