# South West Emergency Evacuation Procedure

Document family: `family.fire.south-west-evacuation`
Version: 1.0
Applies to: South West Region and descendant services
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

## Alarm response

On discovering fire or smoke, activate the nearest call point, call 999 and begin
the service's progressive evacuation plan. Staff must not delay evacuation to
collect personal belongings or records.

## Regional fallback assembly arrangement

Unless an approved site procedure names a different location, South West staff and visitors assemble at the signed front visitor car park.
The senior person
present takes the attendance folder and reports missing people to the fire and
rescue service.

## Applicability

This procedure applies to every Alderbridge service in the South West Region,
including Harbour View in Bristol and Meadow Court in Exeter. A site-specific
procedure may contain additional local facts, such as a different assembly point,
that must also be surfaced when answering a site-specific query.
