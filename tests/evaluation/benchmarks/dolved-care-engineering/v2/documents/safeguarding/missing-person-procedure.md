# Missing Person Procedure

Document family: `family.safeguarding.missing-person`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 July 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

When a person cannot be located, staff check the agreed immediate search areas without delaying emergency escalation. The shift leader reviews the person's missing-person profile, known risks, likely destinations, communication needs and recent events. Police must be contacted immediately where the person's risk plan requires it or there is reason to believe they face imminent harm.

Staff record the last confirmed sighting, clothing, mobility, medicines, search actions and notifications. On return, the person is welcomed and offered support before a proportionate review. Staff must not use punitive restrictions as a response to the incident.
