# Injury Body Map Form

Document family: `family.safeguarding.body-map`
Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 May 2025
Synthetic engineering benchmark material

| Required field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Person and date | Stable care-record identifier and observation time |
| Location | Mark the diagram and describe the anatomical location |
| Appearance | Size, colour, shape and observable condition |
| Account | Record the person's own words without interpretation |
| Action | First aid, clinical advice, safeguarding and notifications |

Do not guess the cause of an injury. Use body maps to record observable facts; they do not replace an incident or safeguarding record.
