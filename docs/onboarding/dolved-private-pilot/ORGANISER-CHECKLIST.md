# Dolved private-pilot organiser checklist

Use this checklist before sending the participant materials. The accepted private-pilot
decision is recorded in the
[private-pilot acceptance](../../operations/dolved-dify/PRIVATE-PILOT-ACCEPTANCE-20261127.md).

- [ ] Confirm the named participants.
- [ ] Confirm each participant's correct workspace, role and location access.
- [ ] Confirm the pilot support contact: **[PILOT SUPPORT CONTACT]**.
- [ ] Confirm the pilot start date: **[PILOT START DATE]**.
- [ ] Choose how the [participant welcome guide](PARTICIPANT-WELCOME-GUIDE.md) will be
      shared and replace **[WELCOME GUIDE LINK]** in the
      [invitation message](PARTICIPANT-INVITATION-MESSAGE.md).
- [ ] When David authorises sending, issue the account invitations and send the personalised
      welcome message. Keep the two messages distinct.
- [ ] Record who was invited, the pilot start date and completion of onboarding.
- [ ] Record who will review participant feedback and how often it will be reviewed.

Do not mark invitations or onboarding complete until those actions have actually occurred.
