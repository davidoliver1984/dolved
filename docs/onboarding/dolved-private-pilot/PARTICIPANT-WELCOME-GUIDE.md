# Welcome to the Dolved private pilot

Dolved helps you ask questions about the policy and procedure documents available to your
organisation. This is a small, invitation-only pilot designed to learn what is useful and
what needs improving through ordinary use and honest feedback.

> Answers are drawn from your organisation's documents. Check the relevant source before
> acting, and let us know when something is missing or does not look right.

## Accept your invitation and sign in

You will receive an account invitation separately from the personal welcome message.

1. Open the invitation link sent to the email address chosen for the pilot.
2. Select **Accept invitation**.
3. If you do not yet have an account, select **Create an account**, use the same email
   address that received the invitation, choose a password and complete email verification.
4. Return to the invitation when prompted and accept it. If you already have an account,
   sign in with the matching verified email address instead.

The invitation works only with the verified account for the email address that was invited.
If it has expired or does not work, contact the pilot organiser rather than forwarding it to
someone else.

## Ask a question

1. Select **New conversation**.
2. Write a clear question about a policy or procedure.
3. Before sending the first question, check the **Location filter**:
   - choose **Organisation-wide** for organisation-wide documents; or
   - choose the relevant registered location when one is available.
4. Select **Send**. You can then ask related follow-up questions in the same conversation.

The location is fixed once the conversation starts. Mentioning a location in the question
does not change the filter. Start a new conversation when you need a different location or
when you move to an unrelated subject. Your recent conversations remain available in the
navigation so you can return to them later.

Clear questions usually work best. Include the policy topic and the specific point you need,
but do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

## Check the sources

When sources are available, open **Sources retrieved** beneath the answer. Select a source
name to see the passage supplied to Dolved, then select **View source** to open the document
you are authorised to access.

Retrieved sources are supporting material. They are not proof that every statement in the
answer is supported by a particular passage. Read the relevant policy wording before acting.

If an answer is unclear, incomplete or conflicts with a document, follow the document and
use your organisation's normal escalation route. You can also leave feedback to help the
pilot team understand what went wrong.

## Leave useful feedback

Under each completed answer you can select:

- **Helpful** (thumbs up) when the answer helped; or
- **Not helpful** (thumbs down) when something could be improved.

Selecting **Not helpful** opens an optional form. Choose the closest reason—**Incorrect**,
**Incomplete**, **Unclear**, **Out of date** or **Something else**—and add a short comment.
Select **Save feedback** when you have finished.

Useful comments describe the problem without including personal details. For example:

- “The answer was clear, and the linked section helped me check it.”
- “This answered the first part but missed the steps I need to follow.”
- “The wording was difficult to understand.”
- “The source appears relevant, but the answer does not match the document.”
- “I could not find a source covering this subject.”

You do not need to carry out formal testing. Use Dolved for ordinary policy questions and
give honest feedback when it is useful.

## Protect people's privacy

Do not enter identifiable resident, patient or colleague details, or other unnecessary
sensitive information. Ask the policy question in general terms. For example, ask what the
procedure requires rather than describing a named person's circumstances.
