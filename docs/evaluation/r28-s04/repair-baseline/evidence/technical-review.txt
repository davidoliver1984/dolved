# REPAIR_CURRENT_DESIGN

Dolved is worth repairing. The evidence does **not** justify replacing Laravel, Python, Qdrant, the embedding models or the overall architecture. It does show that the interpretation-to-evidence contract is not yet reliable enough—and several recent corrections made that contract more restrictive or more dependent on particular phrases.

My recommendation is **one coherent repair of planning, scoped evidence selection and answerability**, followed by a different, small production-path confirmation sample. Not another sequence of isolated patches and paid reruns.

### A. The malformed date: what is established—and what is not

The original question asks how the order of clinical topics **changed in June 2026**. The earliest retained plan already contains `0606-06-06`; eligibility then searches that historical date and reports incomplete comparison scope. [Recorded plan](/private/tmp/r28-unseen-confirmation-20260905-v1/observations.json.cases/v4.case.corrected-b06-02__v2.json:12)

I reproduced that:

- Python accepts the date and leaves it unchanged.
- PHP accepts and round-trips it unchanged.
- The transition normalizer does not activate for this question.

The provider envelope is flattened, validated and normalized in [planner.py](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/ai/app/retrieval/planner.py:410). PHP subsequently checks only that the date round-trips as `YYYY-MM-DD`. [RetrievalPlan.php](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Support/Retrieval/RetrievalPlan.php:92)

**Raw planner output was not retained in the supplied observations.** Therefore I cannot honestly identify the original provider bytes or definitively attribute their creation to the model. The first *observable* bad boundary is the normalized plan. No inspected downstream transformation explains changing June 2026 into that ancient date.

The general semantic requirement is to identify the relevant document transition during the named month—not manufacture an exact day or blindly compare today with an arbitrary day in June.

### B. The feasibility answer is not demonstrated fabrication

I checked the actual answer against its three cited chunks. They support:

- the right to request flexible working;
- compressed-hours arrangements;
- written requests and consideration;
- a trial-period example.

The answer does **not** assert that Meadow Court could accommodate the arrangement. Its `unsupported_aspects` explicitly says that cannot be established. [Answer record](/private/tmp/r28-unseen-confirmation-20260905-v1/observations.json.cases/v4.case.corrected-b03-06__v2.json:943)

However, the recorded prose leads with policy detail rather than answering the central question: **“The supplied evidence cannot establish whether Meadow Court could accommodate this.”** That limitation should be unmistakable, not dependent on a separate field being noticed.

ADR-0023 expressly permits useful qualified answers when some requested aspects are unsupported. It distinguishes generation insufficiency from operational and retrieval outcomes. [Accepted outcome contract](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/docs/adr/0023-define-the-provider-neutral-grounded-generation-architecture-and-contract.md:339)

Therefore:

- this is not evidence of an unsafe invented approval;
- it is evidence of weak central-question handling;
- insisting that retrieval itself must return `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` is not an adequate product-quality oracle here.

### C. The comparison restriction needs correcting—not simply removing

The current code does **not** impose a single-family limit. It allows multiple families **provided each has two distinct versions**.

That distinction matters. Data Protection Policy is a single-version family, so `distinctComparisonVersions()` removes it from the primary scope. I confirmed that its employment-data passage exists in canonical chunk **245**, but its document is absent from the actual eligibility list and consequently never reaches dense search, sparse search, fusion or reranking. [Pair filtering](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Services/Retrieval/EligibilityResolver.php:178)

The ROPA pair survives, ranks first on each side, scores `0.8125` and `0.453125`, clears the threshold, and reaches generation. The generator correctly reports that its supplied evidence cannot answer the employment-lawful-basis part.

This is **not a proven ranking failure**.

The recent correction correctly prevents unrelated documents being presented as two versions of one family, but incorrectly applies that requirement to every evidence need in a mixed question. Post-reranking pairing repeats the restriction. [Final comparison filtering](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Actions/Retrieval/RetrieveWorkspaceEvidence.php:431)

The older contract also explicitly assumes one query with one temporal mode; mixed temporal queries were left as an extension point. That limitation must now be addressed deliberately rather than hidden inside another filter. [ADR-0018](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/docs/adr/0018-define-retrieval-planning-eligibility-and-the-retriever-contract.md:335)

### D. Generic nouns must not become restrictive entities

“Workplace” is present in the first retained plan. The normalizer does not change location references. The resolver then requires a registered match and returns `unresolved_location_reference`. [Location resolution](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Services/Retrieval/EligibilityResolver.php:317)

The registry is doing the conservative thing. The defect is treating a generic workplace adjective as a named applicability scope.

The solution is **not** to ignore every unknown location. That would silently broaden genuinely site-specific questions.

### E. The cross-tenant result is appropriately conservative

All six distinct documents in the safety case’s search candidates resolve to the primary workspace. Its answer neither supplies the foreign prefix nor mentions the foreign organisation or document’s existence.

`NO_ELIGIBLE_EVIDENCE` cannot truthfully be required merely because the expected answer happens to exist elsewhere. The authorised workspace contains eligible, related documents; their failure to answer this particular question is discovered later.

Preserve the frozen record as history. Assess tenant leakage and unsupported claims separately from exact internal outcome-label agreement.

## 2. Architecture and recent corrections

The real path is now:

**Authenticated membership → conversation contextualisation → real Python planner → Laravel authority/applicability resolution → scoped dense/SPLADE search → RRF → reranking → Laravel recheck/threshold/selection → grounded generation → validated citation persistence.**

The production diagnostic invokes that orchestration, with question-only inputs—not expected context injected as the actual plan. [Diagnostic boundary](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Actions/Evaluation/RunR28ProductionPathDiagnostic.php:67), [production orchestration](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Actions/Conversation/OrchestrateConversationRun.php:104)

What should remain:

- Membership-based workspace scope and Qdrant scope filters.
- Canonical chunks, version lineage and authority timelines.
- Existing verified dense-and-sparse indexes.
- Real production retrieval and generation adapters.
- Evidence membership checks and immutable citation snapshots.
- Bounded per-case diagnostic serialization.

- **`8d6dd3a`:** addressed report serialization and persistence compatibility. Retain those repairs.

## 3. Why the tests missed this

The problem is not simply “insufficient test count.”

- Date tests supply the correct date in the fake planner response and assert that it survives. They do not challenge an unsupported date against the question. [Date tests](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/ai/tests/test_retrieval_planner.py:403)
- PHP comparison tests construct an already-correct `RetrievalPlan`, bypassing linguistic interpretation. [Comparison test](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/tests/Feature/SemanticRetrievalTest.php:91)
- Pairing tests prove a valid pair but do not prove that a mixed question can also obtain a single-version supporting policy.
- The diagnostic trims away raw planner output, separate dense/sparse diagnostics, and later selection events. That reduces memory use but weakens causal evidence. [Trace whitelist](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/api/app/Actions/Evaluation/RunR28ProductionPathDiagnostic.php:196)
- Citation validation proves that IDs were authorised—not that their text substantively supports the answer. That is an intentional structural boundary, not a semantic evaluator. [Generation validation](/Users/davidoliver/Desktop/RAG/apps/ai/app/generation/models.py:116)

The reported **3/9 expected-evidence matches** is a useful fixture-coverage observation. It is not comprehensive relevance recall, citation correctness or a product-wide success rate.
