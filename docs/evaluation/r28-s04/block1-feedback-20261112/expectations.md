Mandatory independent review after every case, especially any accepted second-attempt plan: inspect all original requested parts against need aspects, authority, selected evidence and literal answer. A dropped part or a merge that loses a distinct requested part fails the checkpoint even when schema-valid. Do not confuse one complete two-sided comparison with a lossy merge. If no plan is accepted, record coverage as unestablished rather than passed. Record first/second-attempt acceptance separately; a first-attempt success does not exercise correction feedback. Stop for omission, wrong authority, tenant/citation breach or unsupported categorical claim. No selective reruns.

Known limitation (provider-free counterexamples): existing validators accept omitted requested clauses on any attempt; quoting the entire user question does not establish semantic coverage. This checkpoint review is not a production coverage validator and cannot establish a universal guarantee. No coverage mechanism is designed here.
